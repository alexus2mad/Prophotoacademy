## Why

The Food course is now the Academy's main sale and starts on 17 December. The Commercial Content Creator course stops selling, but its material remains available in recordings, so its pages should say so rather than "Набір закрито".

## What Changes

- Put the Food course first in the Studio course list and make it the configured primary course for the legacy catalog.
- Move the Food offering start to 2026-12-17 and update its catalog copy.
- Close Content Creator enrollment and mark its offering as closed-in-recordings. A closed offering marked this way shows «Доступно в записі» in place of «Набір закрито» across the catalog, course page, packages and checkout. Other closed courses keep their copy.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `public-programs`: Food-first course order and a recorded closure status.

## Impact

`content/academy.json`, course ordering, schedule and package status copy, checkout heading, tests. Deployment to staging and production follows a separate request.
