## Why
Managers can create blocks, but the editor groups them at the end of a designed page. A photograph or explanation should appear next to the section it supports, and editors should be able to choose that spot while looking at the page.

## What Changes
Expose insertion points between top-level sections in the editor. Support direct drag placement and a keyboard- and touch-operable alternative. Preserve native section order, explicit publication and earlier pages. Release to staging first.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities
- `site-page-editing`: compose a page at visual section boundaries in the working preview.

## Impact
Marketing page rendering, admin preview, block ordering, Sanity document validation and staging verification.
