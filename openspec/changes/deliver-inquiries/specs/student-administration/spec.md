## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Review inquiries

Administrators SHALL be able to review contact-form inquiries, newest first, with the visitor's contact, program and package context, comment, source, consent time and delivery state.

#### Scenario: Reading inquiries

- **WHEN** an administrator opens the inquiry list
- **THEN** each inquiry shows its stored details and whether it was delivered, is pending, failed or was captured locally, and non-administrators cannot open the list.
