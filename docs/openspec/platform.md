## Reconciliation notes

- Academy owns editable student management. The booking customer card consumes its summary. The text returned by the retired customers endpoint and the ps-booking label in admin settings still suggest that booking owns student management. This adoption records the two stale strings for a product-copy change. The signed compatibility endpoint still supports historical management clients.
- The former time-weighted progress policy and booking-hosted management screens are historical. Current authorized reads use quiz completion, including earlier passing results preserved across revisions. The runbooks identify both the superseded behavior and the present owner.
- The dashboard selects the next non-canceled, non-ended meeting from course/intake session records. It strips the Zoom URL from the overview. The source does not check the individual lesson's active entitlement during this *schedule selection*; lesson, calendar and media reads enforce entitlement separately.
- Health reports availability of its migration sentinel. It does not exercise the active OTP email sender, verify all migrations, run a test charge, deliver Telegram messages, or exercise the deployed booking application. Provider delivery, live merchant state and recovery of missed cross-system payment/refund events require the owning runbooks and environment evidence.
