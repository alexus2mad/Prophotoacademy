## Intent

A distinctive ProPhoto product film showing the actual manager-to-student handoff. The user asked for a good, non-generic ad using ElevenLabs, HyperFrames, and live screenshots or captures. Lead with photography and a human learning outcome, then prove the experience through real software. Ukrainian narration and restrained editorial motion.

## Assets

- Existing Academy public photography, KyivType, Manrope, and original logos
- Live browser captures of the working local Academy/ps-booking sandbox with synthetic accounts and payments disabled
- Real screenshots remain the source of truth; no reconstruction of a full interface

## Customizations

- Close, readable framing of lesson outline, access granted, learning materials, and saved progress
- ElevenLabs narration, custom sound bed and sparse interface accents
- 45–60 second landscape master, editable HyperFrames source and saved capture evidence

## Notes

The local capture environment is labeled as a test demonstration. No real customer records, credentials, payment claims, invented sales results, or unpublished teaching content. Keep existing production websites unchanged. No title punctuation, ornamental numbering, gratuitous arrows, neon gradients, floating dashboard card stacks, or generic feature-list animation. Emphasis on generous margins, readable screenshots, and a purposeful handoff between views.

## Creative selection

Five routes considered: (1) One lesson, two perspectives — selected for concrete continuity; (2) A student's next frame — poetic photography-first but weak manager proof; (3) A publishing-day documentary — clear but too procedural; (4) Reverse chronology from finished photograph to curriculum — experimental tail option, confusing causality; (5) A silent contact-sheet ballet of learner and manager actions — experimental tail option, incompatible with requested voiceover. The selected route uses one recurring course and one photographic subject as its visual anchor.
